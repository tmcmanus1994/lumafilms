# Luma Films — Packages Page Spec (for Claude Design)

Companion to `luma-films-design-brief.md` — all tokens, typography, and style rules from that brief apply here (warm monochrome, Cormorant Garamond + Inter, hairline rules, no shadows/gradients, "Check My Date" CTAs).

**Prices below are the new recommended structure (pending final confirmation) — treat as real for the mockup.**

---

## Page structure (top to bottom)

1. Header — eyebrow + H1
2. Three package cards (Binx elevated center)
3. "Make It Yours" add-ons (5–6 items)
4. Payment plan + delivery guarantee lines
5. FAQ
6. Final CTA section

---

## 1. Header

- Eyebrow (Inter, uppercase, tracked, taupe): `WEDDING FILM PACKAGES`
- H1 (Cormorant): `Packages Named After the Pets Who Run My House`
  - (Alternative if too playful for H1: `Wedding Film Packages` with the pet line as the subline)
- Subline (Inter, espresso): `Every package includes 4K filming, licensed music, your Instagram reel within 48 hours, and lifetime access in your private Luma gallery.`
- One clean H1 only — never repeated (fixes the current duplicate-H1 issue).

## 2. Package cards

Layout: 3 columns desktop, Binx center + visually elevated + badge `Most booked`. Mobile: stacked with **Binx first**, then Boujee, then Rosie. Each card: pet photo (circular or small frame), package name (Cormorant), one-line pet story (Inter italic or small, taupe), price, outcome-stacked list, CTA button `Check My Date`.

Pet story lines are placeholders — Travelle supplies real ones. Format: one warm sentence, e.g. "Named for Binx, the black cat who supervises every edit."

Tagline: `Cute + Short` (keep — existing personality)
- Your 3–5 minute cinematic film — the story of your day, edited to be watched a hundred times
- 6 hours of coverage, filmed in 4K
- Your Instagram reel, delivered within 48 hours
- Licensed music and the real sound of your day woven in
- Delivered in your private Luma gallery — yours forever

### Binx — $3,200  ← "Most booked" badge
Tagline: `Simple + Fun`
- Everything in Rosie, plus:
- Your 5–7 minute cinematic film
- 8 hours of coverage
- Your full ceremony — every word, every vow, clean multi-camera audio
- Drone coverage of you and your venue

### Boujee — $4,800
Tagline: `The Whole Story`
- Everything in Binx, plus:
- Your 7–9 minute cinematic film
- 10 hours of coverage
- Second shooter — nothing gets missed
- Every speech and moment — toasts, first dance, shoe game, all of it
- Engagement film session before the big day
- All raw footage — every second we filmed

## 3. Make It Yours (add-ons)

Section intro line: `Build on any package.` Max one line per item, price right-aligned. No images needed — keep it quiet and elegant.

- Second Shooter — a second set of eyes on everything — $500
- Every Speech & Moment — toasts, first dance, traditions, all captured with clean audio — $450
- Full Ceremony (Rosie only) — every word, multi-camera — $350
- Raw Footage — every second we filmed, unedited — $500
- Rehearsal Dinner Film — the night before, the speeches, the nerves — $750
- Engagement Film Session — $250
- Engraved USB Keepsake — your film in your hands — $100

Removed (do NOT show): 4K upgrade (now standard), Instagram Reel (now included), Documentation of Major Events (merged into Every Speech & Moment), Traveling Fee as a product.

Travel policy is one line of fine print at the bottom of this section:
`Venues beyond 50 miles of Little Rock add $1 per mile.`

## 4. Trust lines (between add-ons and FAQ)

Two short centered lines, hairline rules above/below:
- `Reserve your date with a retainer — split the rest into payments, fully paid before your wedding.`
- `Your sneak peek within 48 hours. Your full film by a guaranteed date, in writing.`

## 5. FAQ

Accordion or simple stacked Q&A (FAQPage schema in the build). Questions:
1. How does booking work? (retainer holds the date → payment plan → timeline)
2. Do you only take one wedding per date? (Yes — scarcity, say it plainly)
3. When will we get our film? (48-hr reel, guaranteed full-film date)
4. What if our venue is far away? (travel policy)
5. What if our day runs long? (overtime policy — Travelle to confirm rate)
6. Can we choose the music? (licensing explanation, collaborative)
7. Do we really get the raw footage? (Boujee/add-on explanation)

## 6. Final CTA

Ink background section, Cormorant line + button:
`Your date only happens once.` → `Check My Date`

---

## Design notes

- Cards: hairline borders per brand system; Binx may use a slightly stronger border or ink-filled CTA to elevate — no shadows, no color accents
- Prices in Cormorant, large — a price displayed with confidence reads as confidence
- Pet photos are the only non-wedding imagery allowed on the site — that's deliberate; they're the pattern interrupt
- No "$1" or policy items styled as products anywhere
- Desktop eye lands center (Binx); mobile stack order: Binx → Boujee → Rosie
